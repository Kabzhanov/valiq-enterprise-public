# Open Methodology Summary

ValIQ Enterprise estimates an enterprise's investment readiness using a 0-1000
index and an indicative enterprise value range.

The public description can mention these layers:

- Financial condition.
- Operational maturity.
- Quality and traceability.
- People and knowledge.
- Digital capital.
- Scalability.
- Owner and key-person dependency.

The public page must keep the legal boundary clear:

- ValIQ is not a licensed business appraisal.
- ValIQ does not promise investment attraction.
- ValIQ shows a range, not an official value.
- Confidence level and methodology version must be visible.

Detailed formulas, internal coefficients, tenant data and private reports remain
closed-source.
