## What ValIQ Enterprise Does

Most owners live in tactical pressure: orders, defects, supplies, cash gaps, staff questions and constant firefighting.

ValIQ Enterprise turns that operational picture into a strategic management signal:

- How ready is the enterprise for investment right now?
- What is the indicative enterprise value range?
- Which actions will increase company value the most?
- Which evidence will make the assessment more reliable?
- How can the company move from owner-dependent operations to scalable management?

The core product idea:

> Do not just estimate the company. Show the path to increasing its value.

## Product Layers

ValIQ Enterprise evaluates seven major layers.

| Layer | What It Answers |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Is the financial base attractive, stable and explainable? |
| Operations | Is production measurable, predictable and controlled? |
| Quality | Are defects, rework and traceability managed systematically? |
| People & Knowledge | Can the company reproduce knowledge without key-person dependency? |
| Digital Capital | Are data assets structured, reliable and audit-ready? |
| Scalability | Can the enterprise grow without proportional chaos? |
| Owner Risk | Can the business run without the owner as the main operating system? |

## Legal Boundary

ValIQ does not replace a licensed business appraisal.

The result is an independent analytical model based on financial data, operational indicators, digital maturity and confirmed evidence sources. It is designed for internal management, strategic planning, investor-readiness preparation and tracking enterprise development over time.

ValIQ does not use or imply:

- official business value;
- guaranteed investment attraction;
- licensed appraisal status.

It shows:

- confidence level;
- methodology version;
- indicative range;
- value-growth actions.
